Nazori: Envelope Calligraphy

Trace beautiful brush lettering onto Japanese gift envelopes.

Lay a goshugi or koden envelope on your iPhone and trace the brush-script heading, name, amount and address at true size. Screen lock, full brightness, and etiquette guides built in. Fonts: Yuji Syuku / Boku / Mai (SIL Open Font License 1.1).

Restore your purchase

Open the app → Settings → Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

Do the people I send links to need the app?

No. Private links open in any web browser.

Can the organizer see the matches?

Matches are hidden while you send links. You can choose to show all matches from the Organizer section — but if you are taking part, that spoils your own surprise.

Someone lost their link.

Open the exchange → Send Private Links → Resend for that person. The match stays the same unless you redraw.

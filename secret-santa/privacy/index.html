Privacy Policy

Secret Santa: Name Draw · Last updated 2026-10-06

Short version: Secret Santa: Name Draw does not collect any data. No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.

What stays on your device

Exchanges, names, wish lists and draw results are stored only on your device. Private reveal links carry one person's match inside the part of the link after the # sign, which browsers never send to any server — including ours (this site only serves a static page). We (the developer, zexuan wang) have no server and cannot see it. If you use iCloud backup, Apple may back it up under Apple's own privacy policy.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details or Apple Account information.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: support page.

隐私政策

交换礼物抽签 · 更新于 2026-10-06

一句话：交换礼物抽签 不收集任何数据。无账号、无统计、无广告、无追踪。

保存在你设备上的内容

活动、名字、心愿和抽签结果只保存在你的设备上。私密揭晓链接把单个人的结果放在 # 号之后的部分，浏览器不会把这部分发送给任何服务器，包括本站（本站只提供静态网页）。开发者（zexuan wang）没有服务器，也看不到这些内容。若你开启 iCloud 备份，Apple 可能按其隐私政策备份这些数据。

购买

可选的 App 内购买完全由 Apple 处理，我们不会获得你的支付信息或 Apple 账户信息。

变更与联系

政策如有变更会更新在本页面。有问题请访问 支持页面。

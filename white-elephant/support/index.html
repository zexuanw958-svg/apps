Restore your purchase

Open the app → Settings → Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

Is this the same game as Yankee Swap or Dirty Santa?

Yes. White Elephant, Yankee Swap and Dirty Santa are regional names for the same open-or-steal gift game. The dice version is included too (Pro).

How many people can play for free?

Up to 8 players with Classic rules. Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription: it removes the player limit and adds Quick, Wild and Custom rules, unlimited saved games, player groups and Dice swap.

Someone tapped the wrong button.

Tap Undo in the top bar. It reverts the last move exactly, and you can keep undoing as far back as you need.

Does everyone need the app?

No. One phone runs the whole game. At the end you can share the results with everyone as an image or as text.

What happens if I close the app mid-game?

Every move is saved on your device as it happens, so the game picks up exactly where you left off.

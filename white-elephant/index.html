White Elephant: Gift Swap Game

Run the gift swap. Whose turn, who stole what and which gifts are frozen — handled.

Everyone brings one wrapped gift. Players go in a random order and either open a new gift or steal one that has already been opened. White Elephant tracks every turn, steal and frozen gift, handles house rules like steal limits and no steal-backs, and shows who went home with what. Also known as Yankee Swap and Dirty Santa. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

白象礼物交换

每人带一份包好的礼物，按随机顺序轮流：拆一份新礼物，或者抢走别人已经拆开的礼物。白象礼物交换帮你记下每一轮、每一次抢夺和每一份被冻结的礼物，内置抢夺次数上限、不能立刻抢回等常见规则，最后告诉大家谁带走了什么。这个游戏在美国也叫 Yankee Swap 或 Dirty Santa。无账号、无广告、无追踪。

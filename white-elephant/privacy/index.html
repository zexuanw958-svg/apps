Privacy Policy

White Elephant: Gift Swap Game · Last updated 2026-10-07

Short version: White Elephant: Gift Swap Game does not collect any data. No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.

What stays on your device

Games, player names, gift names, saved groups and settings are stored only on your device. The app has no accounts and does not send any of it anywhere. We (the developer, zexuan wang) have no server and cannot see it. If you use iCloud backup, Apple may back it up under Apple's own privacy policy.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details or Apple Account information.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: support page.

隐私政策

白象礼物交换 · 更新于 2026-10-07

一句话：白象礼物交换 不收集任何数据。无账号、无统计、无广告、无追踪。

保存在你设备上的内容

游戏记录、玩家名字、礼物名称、保存的分组和设置只保存在你的设备上。App 没有账号系统，也不会把这些内容发送到任何地方。开发者（zexuan wang）没有服务器，也看不到这些内容。若你开启 iCloud 备份，Apple 可能按其隐私政策备份这些数据。

购买

可选的 App 内购买完全由 Apple 处理，我们不会获得你的支付信息或 Apple 账户信息。

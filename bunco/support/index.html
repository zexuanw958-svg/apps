Restore your purchase

Open the app → Settings → Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

Does everyone need the app?

No. One phone can run the whole night. Anyone who wants to can also use the Table tab on their own phone as a standalone scorer for their table.

We have 10 players, not 12. Does it still work?

Yes. When the number of players isn't a multiple of 4, Ghost seats fill the gaps. A Ghost's partner rolls for both of them. Ghosts move tables like everyone else but never pay a buy-in or win prizes.

Someone tapped the wrong winner.

On the Tonight tab, tap Undo last round. Seating and tallies go back to how they were before that round, and you can enter it again.

Can we use our own house rules and prizes?

Free uses the classic rules (Baby bunco +5, no wipeout) and splits the pot 40/30/20/10 between Most Buncos, Most Wins, Most Losses and Last Bunco. Pro lets you change the baby value, turn on wipeouts, set the number of sets, add Most Babies or a Door Prize, and choose how much each prize pays.

Will we lose our season if I get a new phone?

Your nights live only on your iPhone, so they move with a normal iPhone backup or transfer. With Pro you can also export every night as a CSV file to keep your own copy.

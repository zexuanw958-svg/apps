Where do the rules come from?

Public Army publications: AR 600-8-22 (11 May 2026) for ribbon order and devices; DA PAM 670-1 and AR 670-1 (26 Jan 2021, with the March 2025 revision) and Army Directive 2025-18 (12 Sep 2025) for placement. Each rule in the app shows its paragraph. Follow your unit's instructions; regulations change.

The regulation does not say something, or two paragraphs disagree. What does the app do?

It says so. Reference > Not settled by regulation lists every gap the app found, and the coat marks those items with a dashed outline.

How accurate is the true-size view?

Calibrate first: open True size, tap Calibrate screen and match a credit card. After that the rack is drawn at 1:1 on your screen.

I found a mistake.

Please open a support ticket with the rule and the paragraph. Corrections are released as updates.

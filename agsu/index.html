AGSU Setup: Ribbon Rack Guide

Ribbons, badges and insignia for the AGSU coat, with the paragraph for every rule.

AGSU Setup sorts your ribbons by order of precedence, checks the rack you already mounted, and shows where badges, the nameplate, insignia and patches go on the Army Green Service Uniform coat. Every rule quotes AR 600-8-22, DA PAM 670-1, AR 670-1 or Army Directive 2025-18 with its paragraph number. An independent tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. Army or the Department of Defense. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Shakyo: Heart Sutra Copying

Copy the Heart Sutra by hand, one character at a time.

Trace a faint model with your finger, one large character at a time, and finish a traditional vertical shakyo sheet in your own hand, with a wish, the date and your name. A calendar and streaks keep the habit going. No account, no ads. Font: Shakyo Kai, a modified subset of LXGW WenKai (SIL Open Font License 1.1).

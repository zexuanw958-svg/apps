Privacy Policy

Shakyo: Heart Sutra Copying · Last updated 2026-10-07

Short version: Shakyo: Heart Sutra Copying does not collect any data. No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your handwriting, finished sheets, wishes and names are stored only on your device. We (the developer, zexuan wang) have no server and cannot see it. If you use iCloud backup, Apple may back it up under Apple's own privacy policy.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details or Apple Account information.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: support page.
